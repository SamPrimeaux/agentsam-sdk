Model City Missions

Serving River Parish with hands-on love — one home at a time.

Faith Lived, Not Just Spoken

We believe the church exists to make disciples and serve our community. We believe faith isn't just something we believe — it's something we live. That's why Model City Missions exists: to show up for our neighbors with hands-on love.

Since 2017, volunteers from River Parish and across the country have come together each year to paint, renovate, and restore local homes — completely free — for families in our community. This is faith in action: serving alongside our friends and neighbors, making River Parish a better place to live.

What It Is

We believe in living the love of Christ publicly. Model City Missions is a community outreach effort focused on home improvement and neighborhood restoration. Volunteers work side-by-side to paint, repair, and renew homes for local residents at no cost — because loving your neighbor means showing up for them.

This is how we live out what we believe: that God transforms lives through service, that the Holy Spirit empowers us to help others, and that the church is called to be the hands and feet of Jesus in our community.

"Rather than just come to church and talk about it, we do anything we can to be in our community — serving alongside our friends and neighbors and making River Parish a better place to live."

— Kenny Wright, Minister

The Next Generation Leading

We believe God transforms us into His image as revealed in Jesus Christ. Transformation begins at salvation, is sustained by grace, and grows as our lives are increasingly aligned with God's will. We see that transformation happening in the next generation.

Every summer, around 50 youth volunteers join the mission — including groups from River Parish and out of state (Texas, Oregon, Oklahoma and more). They give up vacation time to serve in Louisiana heat — proving the next generation is ready to live out their faith with humility and courage. This is what it looks like when young people understand that following Jesus means serving others.
