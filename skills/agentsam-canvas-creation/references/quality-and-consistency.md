# Shared quality and interaction grammar

Consistency must persist across **blank documents, starter documents,
expert modes, canvas sizes, supported mediums and responsive layouts**.
Consistency is not a demand for one palette, template or composition.

## Constant interaction language

- Compact, near-rectangular precise controls; deliberate hierarchy; restrained
  iconography; selected/hover/focus/error states that do real work.
- Clean + New entry and four directions before generation, without extra
  onboarding funnels.
- One selected object means exactly that object. Show its actual properties,
  targets and contextual actions. An entire assembly is selected explicitly.
- A calm default stage owns the available area. Sidebars, inspectors and the
  Shopify-style AgentSam assistant are context-sensitive and collapsible.
  The miniAgentSam composer preserves the same conversation and run.
- Canonical interactions: pan, zoom, select, focus, drag, context menu, undo,
  redo, save, version history and project navigation where the host supports them.
- Real progress and save feedback, never optimistic claims of completion.
- Legible responsive typography with intentional wraps; do not hyphenate
  interface headings mid-word or turn desktop workflows into mobile mockups.
- Motion communicates state, hierarchy or continuity; honor reduced-motion.
- No giant rounded CTA pills, unnecessary dashboard cards, emoji navigation,
  redundant entry points, injected demo objects or unrelated project media.

## Freedom inside created work

Keep application chrome polished and predictable. The user's output can
choose its own art direction, palette, composition, motion and density, subject
to the selected direction and actual brand decisions. Avoid injecting
InnerAnimalMedia or AgentSam style into customer artifacts by default.

## Quality acceptance

Capture empty, four-directions, partial-generation, manual-edit, assistant
docked, assistant collapsed, save/reopen and failure states. The controls must
be wired to actual authorized operations. Do not approve based on a static
screenshot or an attractive fixture.
