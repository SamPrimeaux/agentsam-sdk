Curated ecommerce catalog, design and fulfillment

Completeful brings a curated catalog into your workspace. Search products, create designs, publish them to your store, and quote or place orders, all from AgentSam or the product pages.

  • Search and read the curated catalog
  • Sync catalog items into your products
  • Create designs and publish products
  • Get order quotes and create orders
Adds to your workspace
AgentSam tools you can mention as @completeful, and a connection page in Settings.
