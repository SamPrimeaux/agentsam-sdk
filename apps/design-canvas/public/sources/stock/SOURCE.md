# Platform stock sources

These 15 interactive design-source exports are supplied by the owner, kept intact and versioned independently of site theme installations. Their dependency files are local to each export to preserve source behavior and revisions. They are rendered only in the Design Canvas sandbox, not automatically executed in customer sites. Customer-facing CMS sections/pages must be materialized through the existing definition and revision authorities.

The four onboarding ZIP exports match previous baseline artboards; the other nine are preserved as independent variants instead of overwriting existing artboards. Account appearance tokens (cms_themes) and site-owned storefront themes remain distinct authorities. See catalog.json for content hashes and R2 object keys.

Two further platform-provided stock boards complete the six-screen onboarding collection: Workspace setup is from the owner-supplied ZIP; clean company B/W style specification is from the authored onboarding collection. All assets are checksum-addressed in the same catalog.
