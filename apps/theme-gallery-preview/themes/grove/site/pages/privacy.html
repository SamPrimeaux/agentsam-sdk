Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 28, 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, and phone number when you contact us or request a quote
  • Project details and preferences when you submit a project inquiry
  • Account information if you create a dashboard account
  • Google account information (with your permission) when connecting Google services (YouTube, Drive, Gemini AI)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer service
  • Process and manage your project requests
  • Send you project updates and communications (with your consent)
  • Provide access to our dashboard and project management tools
  • Enable Google service integrations (YouTube, Drive, Gemini AI) when you connect your account
  • Improve our services and website experience

3. Google OAuth Data

When you connect your Google account to use YouTube, Google Drive, or Gemini AI features:

  • We request only the minimum permissions necessary for each service
  • Your Google account data is stored securely and used only for the features you enable
  • You can disconnect your Google account at any time from your dashboard settings
  • We do not share your Google account data with third parties

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Data is stored securely using Cloudflare's infrastructure
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • OAuth tokens are encrypted and stored securely
  • We use HTTPS encryption for all data transmission

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Disconnect Google service integrations
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain your login session
  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze website usage

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare - Hosting and infrastructure
  • Google APIs - YouTube, Drive, Gemini AI (when you connect your account)
  • Resend - Email delivery

These services have their own privacy policies governing data handling.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
