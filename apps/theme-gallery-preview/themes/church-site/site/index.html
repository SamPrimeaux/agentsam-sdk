We're Glad You're Here

New Iberia Church of Christ is a close-knit community committed to following Jesus and helping others do the same. We believe the Bible is God's inspired Word and the authority for faith and life.

"We exist to glorify God by following Jesus and helping others do the same. We are a Bible-centered church committed to truth, grace, and transformation."

Come As You Are

We're a welcoming community where everyone has a seat at the table. No matter where you are in your faith journey, you'll find genuine people, meaningful worship, and grace-filled teaching rooted in Scripture.

Grounded in Scripture

We believe the sixty-six books of the Bible are inspired by God and are the final authority for understanding God, salvation, and life. Everything we do flows from our commitment to know God through His Word.

Serving Together

Faith isn't just something we believe—it's something we live. Because we've been loved by Christ, we serve with joy. From Model City Missions to everyday acts of kindness, we serve our community with love.

Model City Missions

Serving New Iberia with hands-on love — one home at a time. Since 2017, volunteers have painted, renovated, and restored local homes completely free for families in our community.

"Rather than just come to church and talk about it, we do anything we can to be in our community — serving alongside our friends and neighbors and making New Iberia a better place to live." — Kenny Wright, Minister

Ministries & Groups

We believe spiritual growth happens best in community. Whether you're a man seeking brotherhood, a woman looking for meaningful connections, or someone wanting to serve, there's a place for you here.

Fight Club (Men's Ministry)

Because no man was meant to fight alone. A men's gathering built on brotherhood, faith, and honesty. The Holy Spirit strengthens men to live with courage, purity, and purpose. Second Monday of each month at 6:00 PM.

Rooted (Women's Gathering)

A welcoming space for women to gather, grow, and build lasting connection. We believe spiritual growth happens best in community. Women of all generations growing deeper in Christ together. First Tuesday of every month at 6:00 PM.

Our Core Beliefs

We believe the Bible is God's inspired Word and the authority for faith and life. We believe in one true God—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. We believe Jesus Christ is Lord and Savior, and salvation is offered by grace through faith, expressed through confession, repentance, and baptism.

God & Scripture

We believe there is one true God who eternally exists in three Persons: God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit. We believe the sixty-six books of the Bible are inspired by God and are the final authority for understanding God and life.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is fully God who became human to save us from sin. His life shows us how to live, His death redeems us, and His resurrection guarantees our resurrection to new life. Jesus is the only way to have a saving relationship with God.

Transformation

We believe God not only saves us, but transforms us into His image as revealed in Jesus Christ. Transformation begins at salvation, is sustained by grace, and grows as our lives are increasingly aligned with God's will through the Holy Spirit.
