Rooted

A welcoming space for women to gather, grow, and build lasting connection.

Event Details

When: First Tuesday of every month

Time: 6:00 PM

Who: Women of all ages

What: Community + encouragement + shared life + free food

Rooted is a women's gathering centered on meaningful relationships and multigenerational community. On the first Tuesday of every month, women come together to connect, share life, and grow deeper in Christ — through real conversation, encouragement, and friendship.

Rooted is for women who want community that feels real — not forced. Whether you're new to the area, looking for deeper relationships, or simply needing a place to belong, Rooted is for you. Come as you are. Bring a friend. Become rooted in Christ.

Why Women Love Rooted

  • Engaging, real-life conversations — Share life, not just surface talk
  • Meaningful connections — With women of all generations
  • Free food — Because community is better with snacks
  • Encouragement, laughter, and support — A safe place to be real
  • A welcoming place to belong — No matter where you are in your journey

We believe spiritual growth happens best in community. Women of all generations growing deeper in Christ together.
