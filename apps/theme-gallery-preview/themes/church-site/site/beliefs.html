What We Believe

Our faith is rooted in the timeless truths of Scripture, guiding how we love God and serve our community.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is God's inspired Word and the final authority for faith and life. It is the foundation for everything we teach and do.

Salvation

We believe salvation is a free gift of God received through faith in Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior — expressed through confession, repentance, and baptism. God's grace is available to all.

The Church

The church is the family of God, called to reach the lost, disciple the saved, and love our community as Christ loves us. Every member plays a vital role.

Transformation

We believe God transforms us through the Holy Spirit, empowering us to live holy lives and reflect the character of Jesus in our daily actions.

The Return of Christ

We live in expectation of Christ's return, fulfilling our mission to share the hope of the Gospel until He comes again.
