Fight Club

Because no man was meant to fight alone.

Event Details

When: Second Monday of each month

Time: 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Who: Men of all ages

What: Brotherhood + Bible study + real conversations

Fight Club is a men's gathering built on brotherhood, faith, and honesty. Every man is fighting something — providing, recovery, marriage, fatherhood, faith, purpose. Fight Club is where men come together to support each other, grow stronger, and stay rooted in Christ.

Why We Gather

Every man is fighting for something or against something.

Some are fighting to provide.
Some are fighting to stay sober.
Some are fighting for their marriage, their family, their faith.
And some are just fighting to make it through another day.

But the truth is: none of us were meant to fight alone.

From the beginning, God made it clear: it's not good for man to be alone. We were created for connection — not weakness, but strength through brotherhood.

We gather because the fight within us is too great to face in silence.
We gather because when we open up, we realize we're not alone.
We gather because when one brother falls, another helps him rise.

When stories are shared, something holy happens: walls come down, shame loses power, truth takes root — and God forms men who stand shoulder-to-shoulder: strong, humble, and free.

That's why we call it Fight Club — not because we're looking for a fight… but because we've already found one:

The Fight Within.

"We grow through brotherhood, humility, and the Word of God."

Come hungry — for food and for growth.

Bring a friend. Show up as you are.
