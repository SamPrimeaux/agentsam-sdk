Groups

Healthy growth happens in community. Whether you're looking for deeper friendships, encouragement, or spiritual growth, there's a place for you here.

Why We Gather

We believe spiritual growth happens best in community. We believe God transforms us through the Holy Spirit, and that transformation happens when we gather together — sharing life, encouraging one another, and growing deeper in Christ.

The church is the family of God — the community of believers saved by the blood of Christ. We believe the Holy Spirit gives believers spiritual gifts to be used to help others. When we gather, we're not just attending a meeting — we're building the body of Christ, one relationship at a time.

Fight Club

We believe God transforms us through community. Every man is fighting something — providing, recovery, marriage, fatherhood, faith, purpose. Fight Club is where men gather to support each other, grow stronger, and stay rooted in Christ.

We gather because the fight within us is too great to face in silence. We gather because when we open up, we realize we're not alone. We gather because when one brother falls, another helps him rise. Because no man was meant to fight alone.

When: Second Monday of each month

Time: 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Who: Men of all ages

What: Brotherhood + Bible study + real conversations

Rooted

We believe spiritual growth happens best in community. Rooted is a women's gathering centered on meaningful relationships and multigenerational community. On the first Tuesday of every month, women come together to connect, share life, and grow deeper in Christ together.

Rooted is for women who want community that feels real — not forced. Whether you're new to the area, looking for deeper relationships, or simply needing a place to belong, Rooted is for you. Come as you are. Bring a friend. Become rooted in Christ.

When: First Tuesday of every month

Time: 6:00 PM

Who: Women of all ages

What: Community + encouragement + shared life + free food
