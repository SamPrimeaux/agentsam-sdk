Fight Club

Every man is fighting something — providing, recovery, marriage, fatherhood, faith, purpose. Fight Club is where men gather to support each other, grow stronger, and stay rooted in Christ.

We gather because the fight within us is too great to face in silence. We gather because when we open up, we realize we're not alone. We gather because when one brother falls, another helps him rise.

When: Second Monday of each month

Time: 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Who: Men of all ages

What: Brotherhood + Bible study + real conversations

Rooted

Rooted is a women's gathering centered on meaningful relationships and multigenerational community. Women come together to connect, share life, and grow deeper in Christ together.

Rooted is for women who want community that feels real — not forced. Whether you're new to the area, looking for deeper relationships, or simply needing a place to belong, Rooted is for you. Come as you are. Bring a friend. Become rooted in Christ.

When: First Tuesday of every month

Time: 6:00 PM

Who: Women of all ages

What: Community + encouragement + shared life + free food

Model City Missions

Faith Lived, Not Just Spoken

We believe the church exists to make disciples and serve our community. We believe faith isn't just something we believe — it's something we live. That's why Model City Missions exists: to show up for our neighbors with hands-on love.

Since 2017, volunteers from New Iberia and across the country have come together each year to paint, renovate, and restore local homes — completely free — for families in our community. This is faith in action: serving alongside our friends and neighbors, making New Iberia a better place to live.
