Our Mission

Faith Lived, Not Just Spoken

We believe God created us for relationship with Him, and through Jesus Christ we are offered forgiveness, transformation, and new life. The church exists to make disciples and serve our community—not just to talk about faith, but to live it out together in meaningful, tangible ways.

Serving Our Community Through Model City Missions

We believe the gospel is lived out through action. Model City Missions exists to show up for our neighbors with hands-on love, meeting practical needs and building genuine relationships throughout New Iberia.

"Rather than just come to church and talk about it, we do anything we can to be in our community—serving alongside our friends and neighbors and making New Iberia a better place to live."

— Kenny Wright, Minister

What We Believe

Rooted in Scripture

We believe the Bible is God's inspired Word and the final authority for faith and life. Here are the core truths that guide our community and shape how we live.

God

We believe in one true God who eternally exists in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Each is fully God, equal in nature, united as one. God created the world and sustains all He has made.

Genesis 1:26, John 1:1, Romans 8, Ephesians 1:13–14

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is fully God who became human to save us. His life shows us how to live, His death redeems us, and His resurrection guarantees our new life. He alone is the Savior of the world.

1 Corinthians 15:1–8, Romans 10:9, John 3:16

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit convicts people of their need for Christ and personally indwells believers to empower faithful living. His presence is the seal that we belong to God.

John 16:8–11, 1 John 4:13, Galatians 5:16–26

The Bible

The sixty-six books of the Bible are inspired by God and are the final authority for understanding God, salvation, and life. Scripture is our guide for faith and daily living.

2 Timothy 3:16, 2 Peter 1:19–21

Salvation

All people have sinned and sin separates us from God. Salvation is possible only through God's grace, received through faith in Jesus. It's a free gift expressed through confession, repentance, and baptism.

Romans 3:23, 6:23, Ephesians 2:8–9, Acts 2:38

The Church

The church is the community of believers saved by Christ. We are the body of Christ, empowered by the Spirit to reach the lost, disciple the saved, and make disciples of all nations.

Matthew 28:16–20, John 17:20–26, Ephesians 4:1–16
