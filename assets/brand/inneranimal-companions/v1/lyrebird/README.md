# Lyrebird companion brief

- **Species:** Superb lyrebird
- **Habitat:** deep-canopy
- **Role:** Copywriting & voice · tone and narrative
- **Asset status:** brief-only

The voice specialist. Studies a brand’s language and produces consistent copy, narration, and voice direction across channels.

## Character direction

An elegant lyrebird with a refined fan tail, small field notebook, and a few sound-wave motifs; editorial and expressive.

## Signature action

A fan-tail flourish that shifts through a few distinct voice-note gestures.

This is a catalog brief, not a runtime release. Do not stage as selectable until the transparent animation package, avatars, and manifest assets pass validation.
