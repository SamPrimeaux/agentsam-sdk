**Explicitly not equivalent:** a Three.js prototype, a static "published" badge, a fake file list or CMS settings panel, a run console animation, or a mock preview screenshot.

## Case-study architecture (not a generic card grid)

A compelling public case study follows a deliberate arc, with evidence at the right moment:

1. **Encounter:** a distinct subject and purposeful visual promise, not four interchangeable services.
2. **Question:** the visitor understands the meaningful customer/design problem in human terms.
3. **Exploration:** original research and artifacts reveal how the idea was shaped; proposals labeled accurately.
4. **Experience:** show an original functional scene and thoughtful responsive details.
5. **Capability:** demonstrate the real editor/product or meaningful interaction, not merely a terminal transcript.
6. **Ownership:** show how clients can safely edit, control or extend the result after handoff.
7. **Evidence:** attach actual source, performance/behavior tests, live previews and appropriately verified outcomes.

Keep flagship cases distinct: studio philosophy; AgentSam owned product; Fuel N Free Time applied project; original research/prebuilds. Never imply a client signed off on a concept without confirmation.

## Creative review and acceptance

A reviewer should reject work that is template-like, decorative but unintelligible, technically brittle, disconnected from approved direction, or only attractive in a still screenshot.

Review original thought, narrative economy, typographic discipline, material/color/light coherence, purposeful pacing, responsive behavior, real controls, accessibility, authoring continuity and the fidelity of development handoff.

Automation may verify geometry, file integrity, frame/viewport behavior, build and interaction tests; it cannot unilaterally approve emotional resonance or original art direction.
