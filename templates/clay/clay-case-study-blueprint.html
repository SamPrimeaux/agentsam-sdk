Rules

  • Page title: one-line promise ("We bring your boldest ideas to life"), no hero image.
  • Filter: All / Digital Products / Websites / Branding, each with a count.
  • Grid rhythm: wide card, then a pair, then wide, repeating. In the pair, the left card is shorter (~625) and the right is taller (~750) and shifted down, so the columns never align.
  • Card: media on top (image or looping video), client name, one sentence stating the deliverable ("Web redesign for…", "Brand identity for…").

5. How the pacing works

  • Never the same layout twice in a row. Full-bleed, inset, two-up, offset, pair, carousel, and mosaic rotate. A text header only appears every 1–2 beats.
  • Zoom-in arc. Overview and challenge first, then system and components, then specific features (dark mode, icons, localization) last.
  • One idea per chapter. Heading names the idea, paragraph says what you did and why, the media proves it.
  • Palette cleanser. A text-free full-bleed breaker every 5–8 chapters and again before the exit.
  • Length scales with the project. Meta is 11 sections, Nuant 10, Slack 13, Eden 16, Sky 24 (about 27,000px). Brand projects run longer because they have more deliverables to show.
  • Media is a mix of stills and looping video; the same layouts accept either.

8. Services page (/services)

Measured at 1440px wide. Scale drawing on the left (about 0.32×); specs on the right. Hatched boxes = photographs/mockups, soft ellipses = the 3D (GLB) objects.

Size and scroll

  • Page height: 7,713px at 1440 wide (about 8.6 screens at 900px tall). It's 3 blocks plus the header: hero, services list, industries, then the footer inside the last block.
  • Hero: 641px (0.7 screen), text only.
  • Services list: 4,432px for 5 services, ~918px each (about one screen per service).
  • Industries: 1,919px (about 2 screens).
  • Footer: 641px.
  • Widths change the numbers: your own 1054px measure gave 7,086px.

White vs dark balance

  • Light: hero 641 + services 4,432 + footer 641 = 5,714px (74%). Dark: Industries only, 1,919px (25%).
  • One hard cut to dark at y=5,154 (67% down the page), colour #171921. After the dark block the footer returns to light gray.
  • Nothing else on the page is dark or tinted, so all colour comes from the photographs.

Titles and text

  • H1: "A full-service digital innovation partner", 74px / 81px, left at 5%, about 46% wide, two lines. Subtitle 28px / 39px, 39% wide, two lines.
  • Service row: H2 52px / 57px, paragraph 22px / 30px (about 260 characters, 5 lines), then a list of 6 service names in bold 22px-ish smaller type, stacked, no bullets.
  • Text column is 36% wide. Paragraph and list live in the same column under the heading.
  • Industries: H2 52px, short intro, then 12 two-column rows (label left in white, client names right in muted gray), separated by hairlines. About 120px per row.

Branding row, then Digital Products (the mirror)

  • Branding: text left (x 5–41%), picture right (x 58–94%, 518×679, a portrait about 3:4). Picture shows the brand in use: a poster in an outdoor frame.
  • Digital Products: everything flips. Text right (x 58%), picture left (x 5%). Picture is a dashboard UI on a saturated indigo field, cropped at the edge.
  • Rows 3 and 5 repeat Branding's side; rows 4 repeat Digital Products' side. Strict left/right alternation, nothing else changes.
  • Pictures are square-cornered, with no border, shadow or caption.

3D (GLB) objects

  • 6 canvases: one per service plus one in the footer, each a transparent layer 73% wide × 1050px tall, absolutely positioned behind the content. Models loaded: orbs/branding, flatCircles/digital, squares/websites, doubleCone/content, corners/dev, helix/helix (footer). Two satellites models also load; I didn't see where they appear on this page.
  • Placement rule: each object sits in the seam above its row, about 90px above the picture's top edge, hovering over the picture's top inner corner. Right-picture rows: centered at ~61% across. Left-picture rows: centered at ~39%. It overlaps the previous row's bottom, so objects tie rows together.
  • Size: roughly 330 × 420px, so about the width of a quarter of the screen.
  • Look: pale matte clay-white forms with faint lavender or blue translucency and very soft shadows. Low contrast on white, so text and pictures stay the focus.
  • Shape per service: branding = cluster of spheres, digital = stack of flat discs, websites = stacked panels/cubes, content = double cone, development = folded corner planes. Footer = a huge helix swoosh across the lower left.
  • Motion: I only captured still frames. I haven't confirmed whether the objects rotate or react to scroll or mouse; check on the live page.

Footer

  • Light gray with the helix swoosh behind it. "Let's Talk" as a large heading with email and phone, left.
  • Right: nav in 2 columns (Work, Services, About, Contact | Clients, Industries, Blog), then 6 office addresses in a 3×2 grid.
  • Bottom: social icons left, copyright and legal links right.

9. Briefs for your five images and GLB objects

Derived from the measurements above. Sizes are for a 1440px desktop layout; export at 2×.

Five pictures (one per service)

  • Size: 518×679 displayed. Export at 1036×1358 (about 3:4 portrait).
  • Content: your own work shown in use, each in a different medium so the column doesn't look repetitive. Clay's five are an outdoor poster, a cropped dashboard UI, a phone held in a hand, a 3D character photographed on a desk, and an app UI collage.
  • Colour: one saturated field or a neutral photo per image. The page itself is white, so the pictures carry all the color.
  • Treatment: square corners, no border, no shadow, no caption, no text overlay.
  • Order and sides: picture 1 right, 2 left, 3 right, 4 left, 5 right.

GLB objects (one per service + footer)

  • Render: transparent background, camera angled slightly from above, soft contact shadow.
  • Materials: matte white with a hint of lavender or pearl translucency, no saturated color, so objects never compete with text.
  • Size: about 330×420px on screen, canvas layer 73% × 1050px.
  • Position: in the seam above each row, about 90px above the picture's top inner corner, alternating ~61% / ~39% across.
  • Shape idea per service: one silhouette that hints at the service, kept simple enough to read at small size.
  • Footer: one oversized, cropped swoosh in light gray, left side, behind the "Let's Talk" block.
